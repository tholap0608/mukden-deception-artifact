DAMAGE ASSESSMENT REPORT

Track Damage and Emergency Repair — Main Line North of Mukden

1. Incident Summary

At approximately 10:20 p.m. on 18 September, 1931, an explosive charge, attributed to Chinese military personnel, detonated on the main line of the South Manchuria Railway approximately 7.5 li north of Mukden Station, near the Liutiao Lake bridge crossing. The explosion caused displacement and fracture of rail across an estimated thirty-one (31) inches of track on the eastbound line.
